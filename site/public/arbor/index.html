Arbor

A tree of every window and tab, saved as you go, and never lost. Arbor is a tree-style tab and session manager that lives in the browser side panel.

Why Arbor

Tree-style tab managers earn their place by holding months of context: windows you closed but did not finish with, tabs grouped by project, notes on why you kept them. That makes losing the tree the worst thing such a tool can do, and it happens more often than it should: trees deleted at random, blank windows after a crash, backups that silently stop running, licences tied to a single account.

Arbor is built around one promise: it never loses your tabs. Every change is appended to an operation log before it is shown to you, snapshots are taken regularly, and a Recovery screen lets you roll back to any of them. Everything stays on your machine unless you explicitly turn on Google Drive backup.

Features

  • Side panel showing live windows and tabs as a tree. Closed tabs and windows stay in the same tree as saved nodes, so nothing disappears just because you closed it.
  • Drag-and-drop to reorder or nest; a note on any node; full-text search.
  • Close-and-save a tab, a window or a whole subtree, then restore it in place later. A "close everything and save" panic button clears the deck without losing anything.
  • Crash safety: an append-only operation log in IndexedDB plus periodic compacted snapshots, replayed and verified on every startup.
  • Recovery screen listing every snapshot with its node count and a one-click restore.
  • Manual export and import as JSON. Importer for Tabs Outliner backups.
  • Keyboard basics: arrow keys to navigate, Enter to focus or restore, Delete to close. Alt+Shift+A opens Arbor.
  • Runs in Chrome and Edge on Manifest V3; a Firefox build is in progress.

Switching from Tabs Outliner

Arbor imports the files Tabs Outliner exports, so you can bring your existing tree with you:

  • Tabs Outliner's exported tree files.
  • The onViewClose_lastSessionSnapshot JSON that Tabs Outliner keeps internally. It can be recovered from that extension's page console; the importer in Arbor walks you through the steps.

Arbor is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tabs Outliner.

Permissions

Arbor asks for exactly the permissions below, declared in its manifest source. None of them involve reading page content.

tabs
Read the title and URL of your open tabs to draw the tree, and open, close, move or focus tabs when you act on a node.
storage
Save your settings and, for Pro, the licence state, locally in the browser.
unlimitedStorage
The tree, the operation log and the snapshots can grow past the default storage quota; this lifts the cap so backups are never truncated.
sidePanel
Show the tree in the browser side panel (Chrome and Edge).
alarms
Run periodic snapshots and, for Pro, scheduled backups and the weekly licence re-check.
sessions
Restore recently closed tabs and windows in place, with their history.
favicon
Display site icons next to nodes using the browser's own favicon cache instead of fetching them from the web.
identity (optional)
Requested only when you turn on Google Drive backup (Pro). It obtains a Google sign-in token so Arbor can write backup files to your own Drive. Decline it and everything else keeps working.

Privacy

Your tree stays on your device. Arbor has no analytics or telemetry and makes no network calls in the free tier. With Pro, the licence key is checked against api.lemonsqueezy.com, and if you enable Drive backup your tree data is sent to your own Google Drive. Details in the privacy policy.
