Privacy policy

Effective date: 8 September 2026

This policy covers the BrowserForge website and the browser extensions published by BrowserForge: Arbor, Reroute and CookieSweep. The short version: your data stays in your browser, we run no analytics, and we sell nothing to anyone.

Summary

  • All processing is local. Tabs, rules, cookie lists, logs and settings are stored in your browser on your device and are never sent to BrowserForge.
  • No analytics, no telemetry. The extensions and this website contain no tracking scripts, usage statistics, crash reporters or advertising identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of data. We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise transfer user data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or creditworthiness purposes.
  • Exactly two kinds of network call exist, both under your control: a licence-key check to Lemon Squeezy when you activate Arbor Pro or Reroute Pro, and an optional backup of your Arbor tree to your own Google Drive. CookieSweep makes no network calls at all.
  • No remote code. Everything an extension runs ships inside its store package and is reviewed by the store.

This website

browserforge.dev is a set of static HTML and CSS files. It sets no cookies, loads no scripts, fonts or images from third parties, and has no analytics. The hosting providers (Vercel, and GitHub Pages for the mirror) may keep standard, short-lived server access logs for security and operations under their own privacy policies; BrowserForge adds no tracking of its own and does not combine such logs with any other data.

Arbor

Arbor is a tree-style tab and session manager.

Data processed

  • Titles, URLs and site icons of your open and saved tabs and windows, the tree structure, any notes you attach, and timestamps.
  • An append-only operation log and periodic snapshots of the tree, used for recovery.
  • Your settings and, for Pro, licence state (see licence validation).

Where it is stored

In the browser's local extension storage and IndexedDB on your device. Backups you export manually or schedule (Pro) are written as files to a location you choose. Nothing is sent to BrowserForge.

Network calls

  • Free tier: none.
  • Pro: licence validation to api.lemonsqueezy.com, described under licence validation.
  • Pro, only if you turn on Google Drive backup: your tree data (the same titles, URLs, notes and structure listed above) is uploaded to your own Google Drive account after you grant consent through Google's sign-in dialog. See Google APIs.

Permissions

tabs, storage, unlimitedStorage, sidePanel, alarms, sessions, favicon; optional identity requested only when Drive backup is enabled. Each is explained on the Arbor page. Arbor does not read page content.

Reroute

Reroute redirects and rewrites URLs by rule and removes tracking parameters.

Data processed

  • The redirect rules and per-site allowlist you create or import.
  • URLs of navigations, examined in the browser to decide whether a rule applies. Matching is done by the browser's declarativeNetRequest engine or, for rules with transforms, by the extension's background script. URLs are not stored except in the optional local "what fired recently" log.
  • Your settings and, for Pro, licence state.

Where it is stored

In the browser's local extension storage on your device. If you enable Pro rule sync, your rules are also stored in your browser vendor's sync storage tied to your own browser account (for example Chrome Sync); BrowserForge has no access to it. Shareable rule sets (Pro) are encoded entirely in a link you create; no server is involved.

Network calls

  • Free tier: none. The tracking-parameter catalog is bundled at build time and never fetched.
  • Pro: licence validation to api.lemonsqueezy.com, described under licence validation.

Permissions

declarativeNetRequest, declarativeNetRequestFeedback, webNavigation, tabs, storage, contextMenus, and host access to all sites so that a rule can redirect any site you choose. Each is explained on the Reroute page. Reroute does not read or modify page content.

CookieSweep

CookieSweep deletes cookies and site data for sites you have left.

Data processed

  • Your whitelist and greylist of domains, and your settings.
  • The domains of open tabs and of existing cookies, examined in the browser to decide what is safe to clean.
  • An activity log of the last 500 cleanups (domain, time, what was removed).

Where it is stored

In the browser's local extension storage on your device.

Network calls

None. CookieSweep has no licence check, no update mechanism of its own and no other network activity.

Permissions

cookies, browsingData, tabs, storage, alarms, webNavigation, contextMenus, and host access to all sites because the cookie and site-data APIs require it for every site to be cleaned. Each is explained on the CookieSweep page. CookieSweep does not read page content.

Licence validation (Arbor Pro and Reroute Pro)

Pro features are unlocked with a licence key sold through Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. When you enter a key, and roughly once a week afterwards, the extension sends a request to api.lemonsqueezy.com containing:

  • the licence key you entered, and
  • an instance name made of the product name, the browser family and a random identifier generated by the extension (for example arbor@chrome-a1b2c3). This lets one key be used on several of your profiles up to the activation limit. It does not identify you personally and contains no hardware identifiers.

Lemon Squeezy replies with the key's status, activation count and the purchase details it holds, which may include the email address used at purchase. The extension keeps the key, the instance identifier, the validation result and (if present) that email address in local extension storage so it can show you which purchase is active and keep working offline for a grace period. The key is shown masked in the interface. Deactivating the licence removes this data locally and tells Lemon Squeezy to free the activation slot.

Lemon Squeezy processes your payment and licence data under its own privacy policy. BrowserForge receives from Lemon Squeezy the purchase records needed to provide support and issue refunds (name, email address, order and licence details) and uses them for nothing else.

Google APIs (Arbor Google Drive backup)

Google Drive backup is an optional Arbor Pro feature that is off by default. If you turn it on, Arbor requests the browser's identity permission and Google asks you to grant access to your Drive. Arbor then uploads backup files containing your tree data to your own Google Drive account on the schedule you choose. Arbor requests the narrowest Drive access that lets it manage the backup files it creates. Backups are never routed through BrowserForge servers, and BrowserForge has no access to your Google account or your Drive.

You can stop the backups at any time by turning the feature off in Arbor's settings, and you can revoke Arbor's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive remain yours to keep or delete.

BrowserForge's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the backup feature you enabled; it is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not transferred to anyone except as necessary to provide the feature or to comply with law, and is not read by humans unless you explicitly ask us to help with a specific file.

Data retention and deletion

  • Extension data lives in your browser until you delete it in the extension's options or uninstall the extension, at which point the browser removes the extension's storage.
  • Files you exported or backed up are under your control and are not deleted by us.
  • Licence and purchase records at Lemon Squeezy are retained according to Lemon Squeezy's policy and applicable tax and accounting law. Contact us to request deletion of anything we hold about your purchase.
  • Email you send to support is kept as long as needed to resolve your request and then deleted.

Children

The extensions and this website are general-purpose tools and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change what an extension does with data, this page will be updated with a new effective date before the change ships, and the change will be noted in the extension's release notes. Material changes will also be called out inside the affected extension.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or requests to access or delete data we hold about a purchase, go to support@browserforge.dev. See also the support page.
