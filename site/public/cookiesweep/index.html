CookieSweep

Cookies and site data are deleted when you leave a site. The ones you keep, stay. CookieSweep is free, open source and makes no network calls at all.

Why CookieSweep

Cookie AutoDelete, the extension most people used to keep their cookie jar short, stopped working with Manifest V3. Its successors are few, and the most visible one is closed source and charges for cleaning site data beyond cookies. Cookies are only part of the picture: localStorage, IndexedDB and cache storage persist just as long and are just as useful for tracking.

CookieSweep cleans all of it, for free, with source you can read. It imports your Cookie AutoDelete lists so switching takes a minute, and it never talks to the network.

Features

  • Cleans when you close a site's last tab, when a tab moves to a different domain, when the browser starts, or when you press "clean now".
  • Never cleans a domain that still has an open tab, so you are not logged out of something you are using.
  • Whitelist (always keep) and greylist (keep until the browser restarts) with wildcard patterns.
  • Removes cookies, including partitioned cookies, plus localStorage, IndexedDB, cache storage and service workers for the cleaned sites.
  • Configurable delay before cleaning (15 seconds by default) so quick tab-switching is not punished.
  • Works across cookie stores and containers, and in incognito when you allow the extension there.
  • Activity log of the last 500 cleanups; optional notifications.
  • Popup: is this site protected or will it be cleaned, add it to the whitelist or greylist, clean it now, or pause CookieSweep globally.
  • Toolbar badge showing the number of cookies for the current site.
  • Import Cookie AutoDelete settings; export your own lists as JSON.
  • Runs in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on Manifest V3.

Switching from Cookie AutoDelete

CookieSweep imports the settings file Cookie AutoDelete exports. Whitelist and greylist entries, their expressions and the cookie store they apply to are mapped onto CookieSweep's lists. Export from Cookie AutoDelete, open CookieSweep's options page, choose Import, and review the result.

CookieSweep is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cookie AutoDelete.

Permissions

CookieSweep asks for exactly the permissions below, declared in its manifest source. It never reads page content and has no network access of its own.

cookies
List and delete cookies for the sites you have left.
browsingData
Clear localStorage, IndexedDB, cache storage and service workers for those same sites.
tabs
Know which sites still have an open tab so they are never cleaned while you use them.
storage
Save your whitelist, greylist, settings and activity log locally.
alarms
Wait the configured delay after a tab closes before cleaning.
webNavigation
Notice when a tab moves from one domain to another so the old one can be cleaned.
contextMenus
Right-click shortcuts for the current site, such as adding it to a list or cleaning it.
Host access to all sites (<all_urls>)
The cookie and site-data APIs only work for sites the extension has host access to, and CookieSweep has to be able to clean whichever site you just left.

Privacy

Your lists and the activity log stay on your device. CookieSweep has no analytics, no telemetry, no licence check and no network calls of any kind. Details in the privacy policy.
